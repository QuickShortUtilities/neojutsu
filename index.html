術 How it's made

No base model. No API underneath. Every parameter starts at random and is learned from the data.

  1. 01

    Collect the chips

    Every chiptune transcription on VGMusic, organised by console. Plus NES-MDB: 5,278 tracks ripped straight from NES ROMs, already split into the real hardware voices, two pulse, one triangle, one noise.

    21,352 fan transcriptions + 5,278 exact rips
  2. 02

    Learn what music is

    Pretrain a transformer on the Lakh MIDI Dataset, 178,000 general MIDI files. The model learns melody, harmony, rhythm and phrasing before it ever hears a square wave.

    178,561 files · ~8 hrs on one RTX 3090
  3. 03

    Learn to speak chip

    Fine-tune on the chiptune corpus with a console tag on every track. Ask for NES, Genesis or SID at generation time and get music shaped by that hardware's limits.

    26,630 files · ~2 hrs on one RTX 3090
  4. 04

    Render on real chips

    Output is MIDI, so anything can play it. The site renders through emulated sound chips so a generated track sounds like a cartridge, not a soundfont.

    MIDI → 2A03 / YM2612 / SID emulation
